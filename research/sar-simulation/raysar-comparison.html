Method and limitations

The official RaySAR 1.2 tracer runs on every imported triangle. One output-queue drain fix preserves queued records at shutdown; reflection laws are unchanged. The fixed finish follows the official Step example (reflection 0.5, diffuse 0.3, specular 0.7, roughness 0.005) on every surface. No missing surroundings or flat ground are invented.

A separate audited adapter accumulates levels 1–5, applies an intensity response from the annotated Sentinel-1 Hamming windows, and samples the same native source-height target coordinates as the existing comparison. This is a noncoherent geometric baseline, not RaySAR’s coherent mode or a complete Sentinel-1 processor. Sixth-level raw records are preserved but excluded.
